2. Chep app/ vao thu muc goc Laravel, public/ vao public cua Laravel.
   Can upload public/.htaccess, ke ca khi cong cu FTP an tep bat dau bang dau cham.
3. Xoa build.zip cu tren hosting, dac biet public/build.zip cua backup.shophhp.net.
   Khong dat goi cap nhat ZIP nay trong public.
4. Neu hosting dung Nginx phuc vu tep tinh truc tiep: quan tri may chu can them

5. Chay tai thu muc goc Laravel:
   php artisan optimize:clear
   php artisan queue:restart
   Neu dung Octane/Horizon, reload/restart worker tuong ung.
